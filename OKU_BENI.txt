MacBook Second-Hand Inspector

1. Open EKRAN_TESTI.html in Safari and inspect all screen colors.
2. Open KLAVYE_TESTI.html and press each key.
3. Run CALISTIR.command on the Mac (Python 3 required).
4. Review the TXT report on Desktop; do not publish serial numbers.
5. Ask seller to erase the device and complete internet activation before paying.

No software-only test can prove all components are original or rule out hidden corporate enrollment.
